- Measured baselines from those runs at `--max-context 4096`: affinity `all` 194.06, workers `0` 194.47, host-core `last` 179.81.
- Flag values themselves all check out against the parser: `--ple-io direct|mmap|ram`, `--kv fp16|int8|q4_0|k8v4`, `--host-core first|last`, `--pool-affinity auto|p-cores|all`, `--kv-grow` defaults to off (`generate.cpp:379`), so "omit the flag" is the correct disabled arm.
- Per configuration it: waits for the GPU to free, opens `results/in.fifo` on fd 3, launches the engine, polls for `session is up` with `kill -0 $PID` each iteration, sends one `GEN`, reads the `DONE` line, computes `generated 1000 / decode_ms`, sends `QUIT`, closes fd 3, SIGTERMs, then SIGKILLs if needed.
- Failures are logged as `FAIL_BOOT` / `TIMEOUT` / `NO_TOKENS` with the engine's own last complaint as the reason, and the loop moves on.
- Outputs: `hypercore_max_tuning_matrix.log` (fixed-width table), `results/matrix-<date>.csv`, one log per run, and on a measured winner `max_config_<model>.sh` (env vars plus a `HC_ARGS` array) and `max_run_<model>.sh` (daemon wrapper with `--check`, `--status`, `--stop`, launching at `--max-context 262144`).
- `bash -n` on both generated files is clean, and `max_run_q2_0.sh --status`, `--stop`, `--check` all behave correctly (the GPU guard fires only on start).
